e.g.冒泡排序
```
x=fix(100*rand(1,10));
disp(x);
n=length(x);

for i=1:n-1          %步长默认1
	for j=n:-1:i+1    %初值：步长：终值
		if(x(j)>x(j-1))
			y=x(j);x(j)=x(j-1);x(j-1)=y
		end
	end
end
disp(x);
```
